Profile

Technology is useful when people can actually use it.

My work sits between operations, civic participation and applied AI. I translate real-world problems into practical digital products, coordinate people around delivery, and use AI as an accelerator — with human judgment, verification and accountability kept in the loop.

My path did not begin with an “AI Specialist” title. It began in IT support and digital training, moved into civic tech, then into large-scale telecom operations across African markets. That combination shaped how I work today: structured enough for critical operations, curious enough to experiment, and focused on outcomes rather than technology for its own sake.

I now build and lead citizen-focused initiatives in Côte d’Ivoire while coordinating service-restoration activities in an English-speaking international environment. I am particularly interested in responsible AI, open data, citizen engagement and digital public innovation.
